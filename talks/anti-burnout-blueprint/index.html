The Anti-Burnout Blueprint: Wellness for Solo Founders and Small Business Owners

Presented August 10, 2026 at Forward Fest 2026

Slides Website Venue

Forward Fest 2026

Day: Wed, Aug 19 Time: 4:30 PM - 7:30 PM

This dual-track workshop helps solo founders and small business owners build a business that sustains them rather than breaks them. Facilitators bridge psychological resilience and heart-centered leadership approaches, teaching attendees to scale their impact without the soul fatigue of misaligned growth.

Discussion

  • In what ways has your personal self-worth become intertwined with your monthly revenue or customer feedback, and what external anchors keep you grounded when the business struggles?

  • What is your personal ‘panic threshold’: the precise point where financial fear overrides your boundaries? How can we set clear operating rules before reaching that state?

  • How is your current business model designed to fail when your personal capacity drops? What would it take to decouple your revenue from your direct physical presence?

  • What boundary do you currently have on paper that you regularly break for clients or customers? How can you redesign your onboarding or communication protocols to enforce it automatically?

  • What is one low-risk, repetitive decision or administrative task you make every week that you could completely hand off to an automated system, asynchronous contractor, or predefined standard operating procedure?

  • Let’s come up with a list of 50 cool things to do in Madison.
